🌙 Argostoli after dark — restaurant / bar options

Make Argostoli itself the evening. Choose dinner by mood, then walk the waterfront or Lithostroto and add a wine bar or cocktail stop. These are starting recommendations—not a single prescribed restaurant.

Modern Greek cooking in the pedestrian-center lanes, with an unusually deep Greek and international wine list. Best for a quieter adult dinner where wine matters.
