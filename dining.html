🏡 Stay home — several ways to do it

Breakfasts and lunches are intentionally informal: a stocked refrigerator and pantry, fruit, bread, cheeses, salads, leftovers and whatever people make for themselves. Dinner can be just as relaxed. Nobody needs to join a group meal every evening for the gathering to feel shared.

€

Local bread, tomatoes, cheese, olives, grilled fish/meat, fruit and wine assembled together.

Best for: an unhurried Villa evening that feels local without becoming an event.

€€

Best for: people drifting in and out of the same shared evening at their own pace.
