WORKING PLAN — CHECK THE PRIVATE GUEST CALENDAR

Our guest schedule is a living document

The Villa Astoria gathering runs from June 26–July 24, 2027, with different guests joining at different points during the month. Arrivals, departures and room assignments may change before the gathering.

The detailed roster belongs with the guests—not on the public Companion.

🔒 Private guest-information layer

The current working calendar contains the information guests actually need for coordination, including individual names, arrival and departure dates, and bedroom assignments.

The private master workbook remains the source of truth. The guest-facing private version should be derived from that current plan rather than from an old screenshot, printout or public webpage.

Privacy boundary: individual guest names, exact occupancy dates, bedroom assignments, telephone numbers, email addresses and other private contact information are intentionally not published on this public page.

Why keep “Who’s Here When” in the Companion?

The concept is still useful. It reminds guests that the gathering changes naturally from week to week and gives them a clear place to look for the current private schedule when they need it—without turning the public Companion into a record of who is staying at the villa on particular dates.

When plans change, update the private master first. The public Companion does not need to change simply because a flight, arrival day or bedroom assignment changes.

Before the gathering

Shortly before travel, guests should receive or be directed to the current private guest calendar together with the other private trip information they need for Villa Astoria.

Use the current version: treat earlier screenshots and printouts as historical working copies, not the final roster.
