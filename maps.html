🗺️ A SPOKE FROM VILLA ASTORIA

Kefalonia in Context

Maps, regions and the driving reality of an island built around mountains.

← Gathering home
Villa Astoria is our point of reference. It sits at Ai Helis in Svoronata, in southern Kefalonia, about a five-minute walk above Ai Helis Beach and roughly 20 minutes by car from Argostoli. The island may look compact on a map, but mountain roads make driving time more meaningful than straight-line distance.

📍 1. Where are we?

🏡 VILLA ASTORIA
Ai Helis • Svoronata • Livathos
Southwest Kefalonia

Think of the villa as sitting below Argostoli, close to the airport and the south-coast beaches. This is an excellent base for nearby Livathos outings while still giving access to the rest of the island.

🗺️ 4. Explore by region

Close-to-home south: Ai Helis, Avithos, Svoronata, Livathos, wineries and nearby tavernas.
Argostoli & Lassi: capital, waterfront, shopping, restaurants and Makris Gialos.
Central & east: Robola country, Mt. Ainos, Sami, Melissani, Drogarati and Antisamos.
North: Myrtos, Assos and Fiskardo—best treated as a scenic day rather than three quick stops.
Paliki west: Lixouri, Xi/Petani country and several distinctive wineries.
Southeast: Lourdas, Katelios, Skala and Poros.
