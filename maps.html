📍 Where are we?

🏡 VILLA ASTORIA
Ai Helis • Svoronata • Livathos
Southwest Kefalonia

The villa is close to the airport and southern beaches, with Argostoli to the northwest. This makes the south and capital easy territory while northern Kefalonia remains a deliberate excursion.

Argostoli & Lassi: capital, waterfront, shopping, restaurants and beaches.
Central & east: Robola, Mt. Ainos, Sami, Melissani, Drogarati and Antisamos.
North: Myrtos, Assos and Fiskardo—best approached as a scenic day.
Paliki: Lixouri, Petani/Xi country and wineries.
Southeast: Lourdas, Katelios, Skala and Poros.
