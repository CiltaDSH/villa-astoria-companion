✈️ Getting Ready

A simple countdown for a relaxed arrival at Villa Astoria.
🗓️3–6 months beforePassport, flights, entry rules and transportation.🚗About 1 month beforeRental car, phones, money, health and reservations.🧳1 week beforePacking, medications, weather and final confirmations.🛫Travel dayDocuments, airport strategy and Kefalonia arrival.🏡Arrival at Villa AstoriaFirst groceries, settling in and the first evening.

At a glance

  • Passport valid for the trip and current Greece/Schengen entry requirements checked.
  • Flight or ferry arrival plan confirmed for Kefalonia.
  • Transportation from the airport or port to Villa Astoria arranged.
  • Rental-car driver documents and insurance reviewed.
  • Phone roaming/eSIM plan chosen and offline maps downloaded.
  • Prescription medicines packed in carry-on luggage with extra supply.
  • Heat-smart clothing, sun protection and comfortable footwear packed.
  • First-day grocery and dinner plan decided before arrival.
Travel rules and schedules can change. Items involving entry requirements, ferries, airlines and 2027 seasonal operations should be rechecked shortly before departure.

🗓️ 3–6 Months Before

Documents & entry

Check every traveler’s passport expiration date and the then-current requirements for U.S. visitors entering Greece and the Schengen Area. Do not rely on an older screenshot or travel blog for 2027 entry rules.

Flights & island arrival

Decide whether Kefalonia Airport (EFL) will be the normal arrival point or whether any guest will reach the island by ferry. Send each guest the exact Villa Astoria location and their arrival plan before they leave the U.S.

Transportation

For most adult guest groups, having rental cars gives the greatest independence. Coordinate drivers so the hosts do not become the default shuttle service for every airport run and excursion.

🚗 About 1 Month Before

Rental car & driving

  • Confirm reservation, named drivers, transmission type and luggage capacity.
  • Review insurer/credit-card rental coverage and what the rental company requires.
  • Ask the rental company specifically before taking a car on any inter-island ferry.

Phones & navigation

  • Choose international roaming or an eSIM.
  • Save Villa Astoria and Kefalonia Airport in Google Maps or Apple Maps.
  • Download Kefalonia for offline navigation before leaving home.

Money

Bring at least two payment cards, notify issuers if appropriate, and plan to obtain euros from a bank ATM rather than depending entirely on cash exchange counters.

Health & medications

Make sure prescriptions will last through the trip, keep medicines in carry-on baggage, and bring a written medication list. Travelers should consult their own clinicians for individual medical or vaccination advice.

🧳 1 Week Before

Pack for Kefalonia in July

  • Lightweight breathable clothing and a light layer for air-conditioned interiors.
  • Wide-brim hat, sunglasses and high-SPF sunscreen.
  • Swimsuit, beach shoes/water shoes and a small day bag.
  • Comfortable walking shoes with good grip for villages, caves and uneven surfaces.
  • Reusable water bottle and personal heat-management items.
  • European plug adapters for devices; check voltage requirements for appliances.

Final confirmations

  • Reconfirm flights and rental car.
  • Share arrival times with the hosts.
  • Screenshot essential reservations and save them offline.
  • Check the forecast, but remember that a forecast a week out can still change.

🛫 Travel Day

Keep with you — not in checked luggage

  • Passport and travel documents.
  • Prescription medications and medication list.
  • Phone, charging cable and power bank.
  • Payment cards and some accessible funds.
  • Villa Astoria address/location and host contact information.
  • Rental-car confirmation and driver documents.

Arrival strategy

Guests flying into Kefalonia Airport should collect luggage, meet their rental-car provider or arranged transfer, and navigate directly to Villa Astoria. The airport is close enough that arrival day should remain deliberately simple.

🏡 Arrival at Villa Astoria

Keep the first few hours easy

Settle into rooms, hydrate, connect phones to Wi-Fi, review the house basics and resist the temptation to schedule a major excursion immediately after international travel.

First provisions

Use the Nearby section for groceries and essentials. For late arrivals, plan a minimal first-night food strategy in advance rather than assuming every shop or restaurant will still be open.

First evening

The ideal first night is usually close to home: Villa dinner, Sea Salt if appropriate and open, or another nearby option. Save Argostoli and the longer drives for after everyone has slept.
