🏡 THE HUB

At Villa Astoria

Some of the best parts of the gathering require going nowhere.

← Gathering home
Villa Astoria is not simply where we sleep between excursions. It is part of the experience itself—the place for coffee, swimming, long lunches, conversations, reading, naps, sunset drinks and evenings around the table.
MORNING

☕ Start slowly

Coffee, breakfast and the view do not need a departure time. Some people may head out early; others may decide that the pool is today's destination.

MIDDAY

💦 Pool + shade

In July, returning to the villa during the hottest part of the day can be good planning rather than lost sightseeing time. Swim, read, nap or simply cool down.

FOOD

🥗 Informal meals

Breakfasts and lunches can remain intentionally easy: a stocked refrigerator, fruit, bread, cheeses, salads, leftovers and whatever people prepare for themselves.

TOGETHER

🍷 The gathering table

Evenings are natural reconnection points. Some dinners may be special events; others can be improvised from whoever happens to be home.

NO AGENDA

📚 Permission to do nothing

No guest should feel that staying at Villa Astoria means missing Kefalonia. Reading beside the pool, talking for an hour or taking an afternoon nap are legitimate spokes of the wheel.

MEMORY

📷 Capture the gathering

Take plenty of candid photographs, but also make time for a group photograph during each guest week. The place will matter; the people gathered here will matter more.

A natural daily rhythm

Morning: decide what kind of day it is.
Midday: shade, lunch, pool or a cool indoor experience.
Late afternoon: beach, village, swim or simply stay home.
Evening: reconnect around food when it works.

This is a rhythm, not a schedule. The purpose of the Companion is to remove logistical friction so that nobody has to organize every hour.

One useful question

Instead of asking “What are we supposed to do today?”, ask:

“What would make today feel good?”

The answer may send different people down different spokes—and bring everyone back to the same hub.
