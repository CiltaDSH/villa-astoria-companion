Why the hub matters

The villa gives us enough shared space to be together and enough separate space not to be together every minute. People can come and go, split into smaller groups, stay home, meet again later and still feel part of the same gathering.

Staying at Villa Astoria is not missing Kefalonia.

EASY DAYS

💦 Pool, shade, a book

On a hot July afternoon, the pool and shaded outdoor spaces may be exactly where people want to be. No justification required.

FOOD

🥗 Informal by design

Breakfasts and lunches can remain easy: a stocked refrigerator, fruit, bread, cheeses, salads, leftovers and whatever people make for themselves. Dinner can be coordinated when it makes sense.

THE TABLE

🍷 Where we reconnect

The outdoor table, BBQ and pizza oven give us a natural place to come back together. Some evenings may become long dinners; others may be wonderfully ordinary.

ROOM TO BREATHE

📚 Together, not constantly

Nobody is responsible for entertaining everybody else. Different people can choose different days and still reconnect naturally at the villa.

A SPECIAL EVENING

👨‍🍳 The villa as our restaurant

A private-chef dinner is appealing precisely because nobody has to leave the hub. The setting, the table and the people become the evening.

🤝 The hosting idea

Suggestions, not assignments. Shared meals and a few special experiences can give each week shape, while leaving plenty of room for independent exploring, smaller groups, spontaneous plans and days that never leave the villa.

The point of the Companion is not to fill the calendar. It is to make choices easy when someone wants one.

🏊 Pool life

The pool will naturally be part of villa life, particularly during hot afternoons.

During Our Hollander Chamberlain Family Week: young children are never around the pool without active adult supervision. Use an explicit handoff: “I've got the pool.” → “You've got the pool.” → “I've got it.” Many adults nearby is not the same thing as one adult clearly watching.

🧺 A few house realities

  • Laundry: washing machine available; no tumble dryer. Clothes dry outdoors.
  • Wi‑Fi, keys, parking and rubbish/recycling: the current details belong in the private guest information and will be confirmed closer to arrival.

Keeping operational details separate means they can change without turning the public Companion into a house manual.

One useful question

There is no need to ask what anyone is supposed to do from here. A better question is:

The answer may send people down different spokes—and bring everyone back to the same hub.
