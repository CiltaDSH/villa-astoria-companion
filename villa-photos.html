Villa Astoria is a split-level, air-conditioned residence in gated grounds above Ai Helis Beach. The layout is particularly useful for a gathering because bedrooms are distributed across several levels rather than clustered along one corridor.

🏡 1. Arrival & exterior

Guests enter through a gated entrance with parking for several cars. From there the landscaped grounds and feature staircase introduce the house, with glimpses toward the sea beyond.

Orientation: Ai Helis Beach is only a short walk below the villa; Argostoli is roughly 20 minutes away by car.

🏊 2. Pool, terrace & gardens

The pool area sits within mature lawns and fruit trees, with loungers, umbrellas and outdoor seating. The current rental listing identifies the newly extended 2026 pool as approximately 10m × 5m.

The grounds include fig, pomegranate, orange, lemon and lime trees, plus a dedicated basketball/sports area.

🍽️ 3. Outdoor gathering & dining

This is likely to be one of the social centers of the month: a large patio, casual seating, pergola-shaded dining for ten, barbecue and pizza oven.

Gathering idea: breakfasts, market suppers, grill nights and long post-dinner conversations can happen here without anyone needing to drive.

🛋️ 4. Living room & kitchen

The open-plan main floor combines an expansive lounge with an Italian-designed kitchen and island breakfast bar. Large sliding doors connect the interior visually to the gardens and Ionian Sea.

Kitchen: two refrigerators, freezer, oven, hob, microwave, dishwasher and full-height pull-out pantry are listed by the property.

🛏️ 5. Bedrooms & private spaces

The split-level design gives sharing guests useful separation:

  • Lower level: two bedrooms with doors toward the garden/pool.
  • Upper-ground level: two bedrooms with balcony access.
  • Top floor: principal suite with super-king bed, en-suite bath and private wraparound balcony.

Several rooms can be configured as twins or doubles, depending on guest needs.

🌅 6. Master balcony & views

The principal suite’s wraparound balcony is one of the property’s signature spaces, with approximately 270° views across the Ionian Sea, countryside, valleys and mountains—and toward Zakynthos.

Picture the rhythm: morning coffee here; sunset wine later.
