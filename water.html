On the Water

Let the sea become the itinerary.

← Gathering home
Some of the best Kefalonia days may require almost no road itinerary. A boat turns the coastline itself into the experience: swimming coves, sea views, lunch, conversation and places that are easier to reach from the water than from a car.
SPECIAL DAY

🚤 Private boat + captain

For a group, this is the easiest way to make boating feel relaxed rather than technical. The captain handles navigation and local conditions while the day can revolve around swimming, coves and lunch.

Best for: a special shared day, adults who want low logistical friction, or family week when the vessel is appropriate for young children.
SMALLER GROUP

⛵ Sailing / small-group cruise

A good choice when only part of the villa group wants a full day at sea. Compare passenger count, shade, swimming access, lunch arrangements and departure point—not just price.

INDEPENDENT

🛥️ Self-drive or skippered rental

Potentially fun for a smaller subgroup, but requirements vary by boat and operator. Confirm licensing, insurance, operating area and weather restrictions before booking.

Our bias: when in doubt, choose a skipper. Vacation is a poor time to discover that navigating an unfamiliar coastline is more work than expected.
MEMORY NIGHT

🌅 Sunset + seafood

Keep the morning and afternoon deliberately light. Leave later for a swim, sunset and seafood dinner—either aboard, ashore, or as arranged with the captain/operator.

Ideal rhythm: pool → rest → boat → swim → sunset → seafood → home.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family-week boat test

With young children, the “best” boat is not necessarily the prettiest one. Before booking, ask:

  • Are appropriately sized life jackets available for every child?
  • Is there meaningful shade?
  • Is boarding straightforward?
  • Is there a toilet?
  • Can the trip be shortened if children are finished?
  • Is the swim ladder manageable?
  • What sea conditions cause cancellation or route changes?

🌬️ Weather decides

Boat plans are more weather-dependent than land plans. A responsible operator may change the route, delay or cancel because of wind or sea conditions. Treat that as good judgment, not a ruined day.

Keep an easy backup: nearby beach, Argostoli, a winery, the pool, or a long lunch.

🏝️ Ithaca by water

Ithaca can be a wonderful neighboring-island excursion for guests with enough time. But it should feel like a chosen day, not another box to check. Guests staying only a few days will usually get more from slowing down on Kefalonia.
