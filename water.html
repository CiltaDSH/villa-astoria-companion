On the Water

Another way to experience Kefalonia.

← Gathering home
The coastline changes when seen from the sea. A boat day can mean coves, swimming, long views, lunch and conversation—often with less road time and access to places that are easier to reach from the water.
SPECIAL DAY

🚤 Private boat + captain

For a group, this is the simplest way to make the day about the coastline rather than navigation. The captain handles local conditions while the group decides how much swimming, sightseeing or lingering feels right.

Appeals to: guests who want a shared day with very little logistical friction, or family week when the vessel is appropriate for young children.
SMALLER GROUP

⛵ Sailing / small-group cruise

A natural option when only part of the villa group wants a full day at sea. Useful differences between operators include passenger count, shade, swimming access, lunch arrangements and departure point.

INDEPENDENT

🛥️ Self-drive or skippered rental

Potentially appealing for a smaller subgroup, depending on experience and the operator’s rules. Licensing, insurance, operating area and weather restrictions vary by boat.

Worth considering: a skipper can make an unfamiliar coastline feel effortless; experienced boaters may prefer the independence of self-drive.
MEMORY NIGHT

🌅 Sunset + seafood

A later outing can combine a swim, sunset and seafood dinner—aboard, ashore or as arranged with the captain/operator. It works particularly well for anyone who wants the evening itself to be the experience.

What makes it distinctive: the light, the sea and dinner all become part of the same occasion without requiring a full day afloat.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Our Hollander Chamberlain Family Week

With young children aboard, the right vessel matters more than the most photogenic one. Useful questions for the operator include:

  • Are appropriately sized life jackets available for every child?
  • Is there meaningful shade?
  • Is boarding straightforward?
  • Is there a toilet?
  • Can the trip be shortened if needed?
  • Is the swim ladder manageable?
  • What sea conditions cause cancellation or route changes?

🌬️ Weather has the final word

Boat plans are more weather-dependent than land plans. A responsible operator may alter the route, delay or cancel because of wind or sea conditions.

If that happens, Kefalonia still offers plenty of easy alternatives from Villa Astoria: a nearby beach, Argostoli, a winery, the pool or a long lunch.

🏝️ Ithaca by water

Ithaca can be an appealing neighboring-island excursion for guests who want a broader Ionian day. It makes most sense as a deliberate choice rather than something to add simply because it is nearby.
