🏡 A SPOKE FROM VILLA ASTORIA

Your First 24 Hours

Arrive. Settle in. Learn the hub. Then let Kefalonia come to you.

← Gathering home
The goal of arrival day is not sightseeing. It is to make Villa Astoria feel familiar: find your room, connect to Wi‑Fi, understand the house, get something cold to drink, see Ai Helis Beach and have an uncomplicated first meal. Final house details will be matched to the property manager’s 2027 arrival instructions before guest distribution.
1 • ARRIVE

🚗 Pull in & exhale

Villa Astoria is in the Ai Helis / Svoronata area, a short drive from Kefalonia Airport. Use the saved Villa pin rather than typing an unfamiliar address after a long travel day.

  • Unload luggage before organizing cars.
  • Keep one easy-access bag with medications, chargers and first-night clothes.
  • Take a quick walk through the house before settling in.
📍 Airport → Villa ↗
2 • GET CONNECTED

📱 Wi‑Fi, phones & air conditioning

The private guest edition will include the final Wi‑Fi network/password and any essential house-system instructions supplied by the property manager.

On arrival: save or screenshot the Wi‑Fi information and any house instructions so every guest can reach them quickly.
3 • LEARN THE HUB

🏡 Five-minute Villa Astoria orientation

Locate the kitchen, bathrooms, washing machine, pool access, outdoor dining area, BBQ/pizza oven, parking and key points of entry. The photo tour can help guests orient themselves before arrival.

4 • STOCK THE BASICS

🛒 First grocery run

Do not feel obligated to turn arrival into a major shopping expedition. If basic food and drinks are already at the villa, the larger stock-up can wait until everyone has slept.

  • Water / sparkling water
  • Coffee, tea and breakfast basics
  • Fruit, yogurt, bread, cheese and eggs
  • Wine / beer / soft drinks
  • Simple lunch and snack ingredients
Nearby essentials ›
5 • TOUCH THE IONIAN

🏖️ Walk down to Ai Helis

Ai Helis is the neighborhood beach and an easy first introduction to the Ionian. Current planning treats it as roughly a five-minute walk from Villa Astoria; the exact route should be confirmed in live navigation.

No need to make this a full beach day. Walk down, put your feet in the water, orient yourself to the coast and come back when everyone is ready.

📍 Walk to beach ↗
6 • FIRST DINNER

🍽️ Make night one easy

There is no prize for an ambitious first dinner after international travel. Choose a simple supper at the villa or one of the nearby dining options once everyone knows how much energy remains.

Three good choices: simple supper at the villa • nearby taverna • Sea Salt / Ai Helis area if everyone feels up for an easy outing.
😌 Keep it easy ›🏡 Stay home ›
7 • BEFORE BED

🌙 Set up tomorrow

  • Confirm everyone's room and bathroom rhythm.
  • Charge phones and portable batteries.
  • Put water in bedrooms.
  • Agree only on breakfast—not the entire next day.
  • Let jet lag decide how early tomorrow begins.

The point is to avoid turning the first evening into a planning meeting.

8 • FIRST MORNING

☕ Wake up at Villa Astoria

Coffee on the terrace or balcony, breakfast at home, then ask the question that organizes the rest of the Companion:

What kind of day would feel right today?
