6 • FIRST DINNER

🍽️ Make night one easy

There is no prize for an ambitious first dinner after international travel. Sea Salt sits at Ai Helis Beach and serves lunch, dinner and sunset drinks; Villa Astoria also has a well-equipped kitchen and outdoor dining area. citeturn0search6turn0search2

Three good choices: simple supper at the villa • walk/short hop to a nearby restaurant • Sea Salt at the beach for sunset.
😌 Keep it easy ›🏡 Stay home ›
7 • BEFORE BED

🌙 Set up tomorrow

  • Confirm everyone's room and bathroom rhythm.
  • Charge phones and portable batteries.
  • Put water in bedrooms.
  • Agree only on breakfast—not the entire next day.
  • Let jet lag decide how early tomorrow begins.

The point is to avoid turning the first evening into a planning meeting.
