THE HUB

🌿 Let Villa Astoria do some of the work

Some guests will want a swim immediately. Someone else may disappear for a nap. Others may sit outside with a drink and begin talking. That is not dead time before the trip starts; it is part of why we chose a villa and invited people to stay with us.

No group orientation meeting is required. Practical house information can be absorbed as it becomes relevant.

IF WE NEED ANYTHING

🛒 Keep the first shop small

If the villa is already provisioned with the basics, there is no reason to begin with a major supermarket run. A nearby shop can cover whatever the first evening or breakfast actually requires; the larger stock-up can happen later.

THE IONIAN

🏖️ Walk down to Ai Helis

If energy and daylight cooperate, Ai Helis is an easy first encounter with the coast. Walk down, see where the neighborhood meets the sea, put your feet in the water—or stay for a swim if that is what the moment calls for.

This does not need to become the first “activity.” It is simply a lovely way to understand where we are.

FIRST EVENING

🍽️ Dinner should match the energy

Night one can be supper at the villa or an easy nearby meal. International travel has already supplied enough logistics for one day.

The right dinner is the one nobody has to rally for.

LATER

🌙 No planning meeting tonight

There is no need to settle the week over dinner. At most, establish what breakfast looks like and whether anyone already has a strong wish for tomorrow. Jet lag, weather and morning energy can have a vote too.
