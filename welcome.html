Welcome to Villa Astoria

Together on the Ionian Sea

This month is not meant to be a tour with a prescribed schedule. Villa Astoria is our shared home base: a place to spend meaningful time together while leaving everyone room to choose their own pace.

We gather together.
We dine together.
We adventure together.
We relax together.

How to use the Companion

Use it before the trip for preparation, then use it on Kefalonia as a decision tool. Wake up and decide whether today feels like a beach, a village, a cave, a scenic drive—or simply the pool and a book.

Dinners are opportunities to reconnect, not obligations. Some evenings will be planned; others can form naturally around whoever is home.

The goal is intentional time together without turning vacation into a schedule.

For guests staying only a few days

Do not try to see everything. A satisfying short stay might include Ai Helis, one signature island day, one special dinner, and plenty of unstructured time at Villa Astoria.
