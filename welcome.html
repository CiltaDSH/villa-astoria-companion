How to use this Gathering Companion

Before the trip, use it to make arrival easy. Once here, use it as a menu rather than an itinerary: wake up and decide whether today feels like a beach, village, cave, scenic drive, boat—or simply the pool and a book.

Dinners are opportunities to reconnect, not obligations. Some evenings will be planned; others can form naturally around whoever is home.

The principle: enough structure to make things easy; enough freedom for everyone to have a vacation.

For guests staying only a few days

Please do not feel pressure to “see Kefalonia.” A satisfying short stay might be one nearby beach, one signature island outing, one memorable dinner—and substantial time simply being together at Villa Astoria.
