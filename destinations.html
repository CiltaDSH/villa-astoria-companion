Villa~20 min
Time2–5 hrs
HeatBest later

BEACH PROMENADE

🏖️ Lourdas

Villa~25–30 min
TimeHalf day
HeatHigh midday
MobilityEasy at beach

A long beach backed by Mount Ainos, with cafés and tavernas along the flat seafront. A good alternative when guests want a different southern beach without driving north.

The approach is steep; drive down rather than planning to walk from the upper village.

⭐ Signature Kefalonia

ICONIC LANDSCAPE

🏞️ Myrtos + Assos

Villa~55–65 min
TimeFull day
HeatExposed
MobilityModerate

See Myrtos from the viewpoint first, then continue to Assos for the waterfront village and lunch. This turns the long drive into a complete north-coast experience.

Myrtos: famous does not mean ideal for every swimmer. Conditions can change; treat the viewpoint as the must-do and swimming as optional.

CAVES + HARBOR

🪨 Melissani + Drogarati + Sami

Villa~45–50 min
Time5–7 hrs
HeatLower in caves
MobilitySteps

One of the strongest hot-weather sightseeing combinations: underground experiences followed by a waterfront lunch in Sami.

Drogarati involves steps and uneven surfaces; Melissani can develop queues. Start earlier in peak season.

🌲 Mountain, wine & heritage

COOLER ALTERNATIVE

🌲 Mount Ainos National Park

Villa~35–50 min
Time2–5 hrs
HeatCooler altitude
MobilityVariable

Kefalonia’s highest mountain and a protected national park. Guests do not need to summit: the Environmental Centre and shorter forest walks provide scenery and a very different island experience.

July strategy: go early. The Environmental Centre–Hionistra circular trail is promoted as a lower-difficulty option; stronger hikers can choose longer routes.

CULTURE + WINE

⛪ St Gerasimos + Robola

Villa~30–40 min
Time3–5 hrs
HeatModerate
MobilityEasy–moderate

Combine the island’s important monastery with the Omala wine-growing area. Robola is Kefalonia’s signature white grape and the vineyards cluster around Mount Ainos and the Omala plateau.

Good pairing: monastery → winery/tasting → shaded lunch, rather than a beach day.
Monastery ↗Robola ↗

🚗 Longer days — choose deliberately

NORTH-ISLAND DAY

⛵ Fiskardo

Villa~80–90 min
TimeFull day
HeatBest later
DrivingHigh

The northern harbor retained much of its traditional architectural character after the 1953 earthquake and has a lively waterfront of restaurants, cafés and shops.

Beautiful, but not necessary for every 3–4 day guest. Combine it with one north-coast stop and avoid turning the day into a checklist.

SOUTH-EAST DAY

🏖️ Skala

Villa~45–55 min
TimeHalf/full day
HeatBeach exposure
MobilityEasy

A long resort beach and easy town infrastructure. It makes most sense when guests specifically want to explore the southeast rather than as a must-do from Villa Astoria.
