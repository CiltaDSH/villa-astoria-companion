SIGNATURE DAY

🏞️ Myrtos + Assos

Drive~1 hr
EnergyMedium
BestClear day

Pair Kefalonia’s iconic Myrtos landscape with Assos rather than making the long drive for only one stop. Assos offers a compact waterfront village, tavernas and small beaches.

Parking in Assos can be difficult in summer. Start earlier and keep the schedule loose.

NATURAL WONDERS

🪨 Melissani + Sami

Drive~45 min
EnergyMedium
BestMorning

Melissani is one of Kefalonia’s defining natural sights. Combine the cave lake with Drogarati and lunch in Sami rather than treating each as a separate outing.

LONG NORTH DAY

⛵ Fiskardo

Drive~80 min
EnergyHigh
BestFull day

The northern harbor village retained much of its older Venetian character after the 1953 earthquake and has a lively waterfront of restaurants, cafés and shops.

Worth doing, but not on a day when the group wants a relaxed schedule. Consider combining with a north-coast stop rather than rushing.

🌊 Avithos

Drive~10 min
EnergyLow
BestEasy half day

A close-to-home beach day that pairs naturally with lunch or dinner, making it particularly useful during the hotter part of the trip when a long drive is less appealing.
