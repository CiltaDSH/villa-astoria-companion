3–6 MONTHS BEFORE

🗓️ Documents, flights & transportation

  • Check every traveler’s passport expiration date and then-current Greece/Schengen entry requirements.
  • Confirm flights and whether arrival will be through Kefalonia Airport (EFL) or ferry.
  • Send guests the exact Villa Astoria location and arrival plan.
  • Coordinate rental cars and drivers so guests retain independence and the hosts do not become the default shuttle service.
EFL → Villa route ↗
ABOUT 1 MONTH BEFORE

🚗 Cars, phones, money & health

  • Confirm rental car, named drivers, transmission type and luggage capacity.
  • Review rental insurance and ask before taking any rental car on an inter-island ferry.
  • Choose international roaming or an eSIM and download Kefalonia offline maps.
  • Bring at least two payment cards and plan to use bank ATMs for euros.
  • Make sure prescriptions last through the trip; keep medicines in carry-on baggage and bring a written medication list.
1 WEEK BEFORE

🧳 Pack for July

  • Lightweight breathable clothing, hat, sunglasses and high-SPF sunscreen.
  • Swimsuit, beach/water shoes and a small day bag.
  • Comfortable walking shoes with good grip.
  • Reusable water bottle and personal heat-management items.
  • European plug adapters; check voltage requirements for personal appliances.
  • Reconfirm flights, rental car and arrival times; save key reservations offline.
TRAVEL DAY

🛫 Keep these with you

  • Passport and travel documents.
  • Prescription medications and medication list.
  • Phone, charging cable and power bank.
  • Payment cards and accessible funds.
  • Villa Astoria location/contact information.
  • Rental-car confirmation and driver documents.

After landing at EFL, collect luggage, meet the rental-car provider or arranged transfer and head directly to Villa Astoria. Keep arrival day intentionally simple.

ARRIVAL

🏡 First hours at Villa Astoria

Settle into rooms, hydrate, connect to Wi-Fi and review the house basics before planning anything ambitious.

  • Use the Companion’s Nearby information for first groceries and essentials.
  • For a late arrival, have a simple first-night food plan rather than assuming shops remain open.
  • Keep the first evening close to home; save Argostoli and longer drives until after everyone has slept.
