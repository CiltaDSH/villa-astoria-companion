Getting Ready

One goal: arrive ready to relax

Our time together at Villa Astoria begins in summer 2027, but the easiest arrivals start months earlier. Work through this page gradually. By departure day, each household should be able to travel, communicate, navigate, manage medications and money, and reach Villa Astoria independently.

The goal: arrive, unpack, exhale—and enjoy Greece.

3–6 MONTHS BEFORE

🗓️ Settle the big pieces

🛂 Passport

Check each traveler’s passport now. Confirm that the name matches airline reservations and that the passport meets the then-current validity rules for Greece and the Schengen Area.

🇪🇺 European entry rules

Recheck the official requirements that apply in summer 2027, including any operational EES or ETIAS requirements. Implementation dates have changed before, so use official sources close to travel rather than an old checklist.

✈️ Flights

Most guests will likely arrive either through Athens and connect to Kefalonia Airport (EFL), or connect through another European hub with seasonal service to Kefalonia. Ferries make more sense when Kefalonia is part of a broader Greek itinerary.

🚗 Independence on the island

  • Coordinate rental cars and named drivers so every outing does not depend on Danny and Priscilla.
  • Reserve early if you need an automatic transmission, larger vehicle, or child safety seats.
  • Confirm current driver-license / International Driving Permit requirements and rental insurance terms.

Why: the hub-and-spokes idea works best when different people can follow different spokes and meet again at Villa Astoria.

4–8 WEEKS BEFORE

🚗 Make yourself travel-ready

📱 Phone & maps

Choose international roaming or an eSIM. Download Kefalonia for offline use in your preferred mapping app and save the Villa Astoria location.

💶 Money

Bring at least two payment cards. Cards are widely useful, but some euros are convenient. When an ATM or terminal offers USD or EUR, choose EUR and let your own bank/card network handle conversion.

💊 Medications

Make sure prescriptions cover the full trip plus a reasonable delay buffer. Keep essential medicines in carry-on baggage and carry a current medication list.

🧾 Insurance

Review overseas medical coverage, trip interruption, and emergency medical transportation/evacuation. Avoid paying for duplicate benefits you already have.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family-week vehicles

A vehicle advertised for seven passengers may not comfortably carry seven people plus luggage and child seats. Confirm actual seating, luggage space and appropriate restraints before booking.

Health note: individual vaccination, medication and medical-travel questions belong with each traveler’s clinician or pharmacist. The Companion provides practical orientation, not personal medical advice.

1 WEEK BEFORE

🧳 Pack for the way we’ll actually live

👕 Clothing

Lightweight breathable clothes, swimsuits, casual evening wear, hat, sandals, and comfortable walking shoes. Kefalonia is relaxed; comfort should dominate.

🏖️ Beach

Sunglasses, sunscreen, reusable water bottle, small beach bag and water shoes. Many beautiful beaches are pebble rather than sand.

🔌 Electronics

Bring European plug adapters. Greece uses 230V power; check voltage compatibility before using U.S. hair dryers or other heating appliances.

☀️ Heat

Bring the sun-protection and hydration items you will actually use. Our preferred July rhythm is morning activity, shaded midday, and late-afternoon/evening activity.

Laundry: Villa Astoria has a washing machine but no tumble dryer. Clothes can dry outdoors in the Kefalonian sun—another reason to pack lighter than you think you need.

Final week checklist

  • Check flight schedules and airline apps.
  • Download offline maps and save Villa Astoria.
  • Confirm rental car / transfer arrangements.
  • Save important contacts offline.
  • Pack medications in carry-on luggage.
  • Pack passport, cards, phone, chargers and adapters.
  • Check current Kefalonia weather shortly before departure.

TRAVEL DAY

🛫 Keep the irreplaceable things with you

If checked luggage disappears temporarily, these should not disappear with it:

  • 🛂 Passport and travel documents
  • 💊 Medication and medication list
  • 📱 Phone, charging cable and power bank
  • 💳 Wallet and payment cards
  • 🏡 Villa Astoria location/contact information
  • 🚗 Rental confirmation and driver documents

After landing at EFL, collect luggage, meet the rental-car provider or arranged transfer and head to the villa. If connecting through Athens after an overnight U.S. flight, allow generous connection time rather than beginning the vacation with a sprint through the airport.

Optional Athens pause: guests who want a gentler transition—or who have never visited Athens—may prefer to arrive in Greece a day or two early rather than treating Athens only as a connection.
ARRIVAL AT THE HUB

🏡 You made it. Stop traveling.

Once you reach Villa Astoria, the preparation layer has done its job. Find your room. Connect to Wi‑Fi. Drink something cold. Walk outside. Look at the sea.

  • Do not feel obligated to make a supermarket stop between the airport and villa unless you want to.
  • Keep the first meal uncomplicated.
  • Take a swim or walk to Ai Helis if there is energy.
  • Do not start planning the whole stay on arrival night.
The transition: preparation ends here. The Companion now becomes a menu of possibilities.

A SIMPLE REMINDER

☀️ The Greek summer rhythm

Morning: beach, boating, walking or exploring.
Midday: shade, lunch, villa, pool, reading or siesta.
Late afternoon & evening: swim again, wander a village, have drinks and dinner.

You do not need to conquer Kefalonia between noon and 4 p.m. Sometimes the smartest itinerary is to slow down.

Important: travel rules, airline and ferry schedules, business hours and seasonal operations can change. Recheck these shortly before departure and use current official sources for anything time-sensitive.
