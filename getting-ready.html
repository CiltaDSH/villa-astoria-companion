GETTING HERE

✈️ Athens → Kefalonia Airport (EFL)

EFL → Villa Astoria: a short local drive

For guests whose international itinerary brings them through Athens, our preferred onward route is to fly from Athens to Kefalonia International Airport “Anna Pollatou” (EFL). Once the 2027 schedules for your dates are available, it is worth arranging this flight early rather than leaving the Athens–Kefalonia segment until the rest of the trip is already fixed.

Allow a comfortable connection in Athens, especially if the Athens and Kefalonia flights are on separate tickets or checked baggage may need to be collected and rechecked. The final schedule should always be confirmed with the operating airline.

Our recommendation: if you are coming through Athens, fly to EFL if at all practical. It is usually the simplest way to finish the journey to Villa Astoria.

Why we do not favor the ferry for arrival

Ferry travel remains an option for a broader Greek itinerary, but it is not our preferred way to reach the gathering. Reaching the mainland ferry port from Athens adds another transfer before the sea crossing, and several of our guests have told us they are susceptible to seasickness. For them in particular, the ferry may be an uncomfortable way to begin or end the visit.

If a ferry genuinely dovetails with travel elsewhere in Greece, it can still make sense; otherwise, we suggest planning around the Athens–EFL flight.

CARS + VILLA PARKING

🚗 Do you need your own rental car?

A car makes Kefalonia much easier to explore, but every guest—or every couple—does not necessarily need a separate rental. Before booking one, please look at who else will be staying at Villa Astoria during the same dates and consider whether sharing a car would work comfortably.

Important parking limit: Villa Astoria has three parking spaces total. Danny and Priscilla will be using one throughout the month, leaving room for a maximum of two guest rental cars at any one time.

Coordinate before reserving

Please discuss rental-car plans with us and with guests whose stays overlap before making a nonrefundable reservation. Two guest cars should provide useful independence while respecting the villa's parking limit. Sharing may be especially sensible when guests expect to explore together much of the time.

Reserve deliberately

If an automatic transmission, a larger vehicle or child seats matter, book early enough that those requirements—not simply the lowest rate—drive the choice. Passenger and luggage space matter more than the nominal seat count.

Kefalonia rewards looking at drive time rather than mileage alone. Roads can be winding, and a north-island outing is a different proposition from dinner or a beach near the villa. The goal is enough transportation for independence without more cars than Villa Astoria can accommodate.

KEFALONIA-SPECIFIC, NOT A PACKING LIST

☀️ A few things the island rewards

July suggests light clothing, serious sun protection and footwear that works on uneven ground and pebble beaches. Beyond that, everyone already knows what they like to travel with.

One Villa detail worth knowing: there is a washing machine but no tumble dryer; clothes dry outdoors. That makes packing light particularly easy.

The other useful preparation is mental rather than material: distances can take longer than they look, midday can be hot, and the island often feels best when a day has room in it.
