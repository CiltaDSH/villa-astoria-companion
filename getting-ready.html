GETTING HERE

✈️ Kefalonia Airport (EFL)

EFL → Villa Astoria: a short local drive

Most guests arriving by air will finish the journey at Kefalonia Airport “Anna Pollatou” (EFL). Athens is the obvious Greek connection, while summer schedules also bring seasonal service from a number of European cities. The useful point is not to predict the 2027 timetable now—it is to revisit the actual schedule once flights for our dates are firm.

Guests combining Kefalonia with a broader Greek itinerary may reasonably arrive by ferry instead. That is a travel-design choice rather than a preferred way to reach the gathering.

THE ISLAND WORKS BETTER THIS WAY

🚗 Independent wheels

Villa Astoria is close to the airport, Ai Helis and local amenities, but much of what makes Kefalonia memorable is spread across a large, mountainous island. A car gives guests the freedom to choose different spokes and return to the same hub without turning Danny and Priscilla into drivers or dispatchers.

Reserve deliberately

If an automatic transmission, a larger vehicle or child seats matter, book early enough that those requirements—not simply the lowest rate—drive the choice.

Think in drive time

Kefalonia rewards looking at the route, not just the mileage. Roads can be winding, and a north-island outing is a different proposition from dinner or a beach near the villa.

During Our Hollander Chamberlain Family Week, actual passenger space matters more than the number printed in a rental category: adults, four grandchildren, child restraints and luggage quickly change what “seven seats” means.

KEFALONIA-SPECIFIC, NOT A PACKING LIST

☀️ A few things the island rewards

July suggests light clothing, serious sun protection and footwear that works on uneven ground and pebble beaches. Beyond that, everyone already knows what they like to travel with.

One Villa detail worth knowing: there is a washing machine but no tumble dryer; clothes dry outdoors. That makes packing light particularly easy.

The other useful preparation is mental rather than material: distances can take longer than they look, midday can be hot, and the island often feels best when a day has room in it.
