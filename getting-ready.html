Getting Ready

One goal: arrive ready to relax

This is not a giant travel checklist. It covers the few things worth settling before departure so that the gathering does not begin with avoidable logistics.

Each guest household should be able to arrive independently, communicate, navigate, manage medications and money, and know how to reach Villa Astoria.

3–6 MONTHS BEFORE

🗓️ Get the big pieces settled

  • Check each traveler’s passport expiration date and the then-current Greece/Schengen entry requirements.
  • Confirm flights and whether arrival will be through Kefalonia Airport (EFL) or ferry.
  • Save the exact Villa Astoria location and arrival information.
  • Coordinate rental cars and named drivers so households have reasonable independence during their stay.

Why: the hub-and-spokes idea works best when every outing does not depend on the hosts driving everyone.

EFL → Villa route ↗
ABOUT 1 MONTH BEFORE

🚗 Make yourself independent

  • Confirm rental car, drivers, transmission type and luggage capacity.
  • Review rental insurance and ask before taking any rental vehicle on an inter-island ferry.
  • Choose international roaming or an eSIM and download Kefalonia offline maps.
  • Bring at least two payment cards and plan for access to euros.
  • Make sure prescriptions cover the entire trip; keep medicines in carry-on baggage and bring a written medication list.
1 WEEK BEFORE

🧳 Pack for the way we’ll actually live

  • Lightweight breathable clothing, hat, sunglasses and high-SPF sunscreen.
  • Swimsuit, beach/water shoes and a small day bag.
  • Comfortable walking shoes with good grip.
  • Reusable water bottle and personal heat-management items.
  • European plug adapters; check voltage requirements for personal appliances.
  • Save key reservations, Villa Astoria location and important numbers offline.
Do not overpack for formality. The gathering is centered on a villa, beaches, villages and relaxed meals. Bring something you enjoy wearing for a special dinner, but comfort should dominate.
TRAVEL DAY

🛫 Keep the essentials with you

  • Passport and travel documents.
  • Prescription medications and medication list.
  • Phone, charging cable and power bank.
  • Payment cards and accessible funds.
  • Villa Astoria location/contact information.
  • Rental-car confirmation and driver documents.

After landing at EFL, collect luggage, meet the rental-car provider or arranged transfer and head to the villa. Arrival day should be one of the easiest days of the trip.

ARRIVAL AT THE HUB

🏡 You made it. Stop traveling.

Settle into your room, hydrate, connect to Wi-Fi, unpack what you need and learn the few house basics that matter.

  • Have water and simple food available rather than immediately launching a major grocery expedition.
  • Take a swim if it feels good.
  • Walk to Ai Helis if there is energy.
  • Keep the first evening close to home.
  • Do not start planning the whole stay on arrival night.
The transition: once you arrive at Villa Astoria, the preparation layer has done its job. Now the Gathering Companion becomes a menu of possibilities.
