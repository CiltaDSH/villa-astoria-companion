🛒 Groceries

Villa → local Svoronata shops: roughly 10–20 min walk / a few min drive

Sagittarius Cava Market is a practical nearby option for day-to-day groceries, drinks, chilled items and forgotten essentials. Minimarket Tiritompas is another useful local choice for quick top-ups.

Nearby shops are well suited to: breakfast items, fruit, drinks, cheeses, sandwich or salad ingredients and snacks. A larger Argostoli-area supermarket may be useful when a more substantial stock-up is wanted.

💊 Pharmacy

Villa → Argostoli: about 15–20 min drive

For routine needs, Argostoli offers the strongest choice of pharmacies. Healthy Habit Pharmacy — K. Galiatsatou is one useful reference point in town; for nights, Sundays and holidays, check which pharmacy is currently on duty rather than relying on ordinary hours.

⛽ Fuel

Villa → Svoronata fuel: short local drive

PAN oil is a convenient Svoronata fuel stop and can be useful before or after a longer island drive.

For longer north-island days: Myrtos, Assos and Fiskardo involve substantially more driving than the nearby beaches or Argostoli, so the fuel gauge is worth including in the day’s logistics.

🏧 Cash

Villa → bank ATMs in Argostoli: about 15–20 min drive

Cards are widely useful; some euros can still be convenient for smaller purchases. Bank-affiliated ATMs in Argostoli are easy to locate when needed.

At an ATM or card terminal: travelers who prefer their own bank or card network to handle currency conversion can choose EUR rather than a displayed home-currency conversion.
