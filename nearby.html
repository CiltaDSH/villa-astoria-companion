💊 Pharmacy

Villa → Argostoli: about 15–20 min drive

For routine needs, Argostoli offers the strongest choice of pharmacies. Healthy Habit Pharmacy — K. Galiatsatou is one current reference point in town, not a guaranteed 2027 recommendation. For an urgent but non-emergency need at night, on Sunday or on a holiday, check the current on-duty pharmacy rather than assuming any named pharmacy is open or relying on its ordinary hours.

📱 Mobile service, Wi‑Fi & maps

If an existing U.S. carrier offers a simple, reasonably priced international plan, using it may be the easiest choice. A travel eSIM can be a good alternative when it better fits the individual phone, plan or expected data use. There is no reason for everyone to use the same solution.

Whichever option you choose, download an offline map of Kefalonia. Mobile service is established across Greece, but coverage on a mountainous island can vary by precise location; remote roads, beaches and coves are where an offline map becomes particularly useful.

Worth doing before travel: save Villa Astoria in the preferred map app and download Kefalonia for offline navigation.
