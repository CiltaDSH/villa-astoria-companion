🛒 Groceries

Villa → local Svoronata shops: roughly 10–20 min walk / a few min drive

Sagittarius Cava Market is a practical nearby default for day-to-day groceries, drinks, chilled items and forgotten holiday essentials. Minimarket Tiritompas is another useful local option for quick top-ups.

Villa strategy: use the local shops for breakfast items, fruit, drinks, cheeses, sandwich/salad ingredients and snacks; save a larger Argostoli-area supermarket for a substantial stock-up if needed.

Dolce Far Niente is a nearby café-bakery option for coffee, breakfast, cakes and pastries. It works well as a short morning outing rather than another errand that requires a drive into Argostoli.

💊 Pharmacy

Villa → Argostoli: about 15–20 min drive

For routine needs, Argostoli offers the strongest choice of pharmacies. Healthy Habit Pharmacy — K. Galiatsatou is one useful reference point in town; for nights, Sundays and holidays, check which pharmacy is currently on duty rather than relying on ordinary hours.

⛽ Fuel

Villa → Svoronata fuel: short local drive

PAN oil is a convenient Svoronata fuel stop. It is useful for a quick top-up before a longer island day; check the posted fuel grade and price at the pump as you would anywhere.

Easy rule: if tomorrow is Myrtos, Assos or Fiskardo, check the fuel gauge tonight.

🏧 Cash

Villa → bank ATMs in Argostoli: about 15–20 min drive

Cards are widely useful, but keep some euros for small purchases. Prefer an ATM attached to a Greek bank when practical rather than an independent tourist-area machine.

Currency choice: when offered USD or EUR at an ATM or card terminal, choose EUR and let your own bank/card network handle conversion.
