Family Week

Grandparents, parents and children sharing one home base—without needing one schedule.

← Gathering home

The goal is not to entertain the children every minute. It is to give three generations room to enjoy one another.

The villa is especially valuable this week. Pool time, naps, simple meals and familiar surroundings give the youngest guests an easy home base—and give the adults permission to split into smaller groups when that works better.

BEST EASY BEACHES

🏖️ Ammes + Avithos

For young children, practical beaches matter more than postcard fame. Ammes and Avithos make easier family choices than building the day around Myrtos.

Family advantage: keep the outing short enough that tired children—and tired adults—can be back at the pool without turning the return trip into an expedition.

EASY OUTING

🐢 Argostoli Lagoon

A calm morning or late-afternoon alternative to another beach. Look for Caretta-caretta turtles, stroll the waterfront and let the adults enjoy a café without committing the family to a long sightseeing day.

FAMILY ADVENTURE

🫏 Donkey outing near Sami

A countryside donkey experience can be a memorable change of pace. Keep it short for younger children and confirm the operator’s current age, riding and weight rules before booking.

HEAT OPTION

🪨 Melissani + Drogarati

The caves add novelty without requiring a full day in direct sun. Pair them with lunch in Sami rather than stacking several distant stops.

Note: Drogarati involves steps and uneven surfaces. Decide based on the youngest children and anyone with mobility concerns.

SPECIAL DAY

⛵ Family boat day

A crewed family cruise can work particularly well for a multigenerational group because swimming, scenery, lunch and shade happen without repeated car loading and unloading.

Prioritize: shade, easy boarding, toilet facilities, child flotation gear and a captain/crew rather than a self-drive boat.

ICONIC PHOTO

📷 Myrtos: photograph first

Use the viewpoint for the iconic family photograph, but treat swimming there as optional. For an easier family swim, choose a more practical southern or organized beach.

☀️ The family-week rhythm

Morning: one outing while everyone is fresh.
Midday: lunch, pool, naps and shade.
Late afternoon: second swim or simple activity if people want it.
Evening: reconnect when it works.

The children do not need an attraction every day. Villa Astoria itself is one of the family experiences.

❤️ The memory we are really making

The grandchildren may remember the cave or the boat. They may just as easily remember jumping into the pool with their cousins, breakfast with grandparents, staying up later than usual, or everyone laughing around the table.

That unstructured time is not what happens between the activities. It is part of why we are gathering.
