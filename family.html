BEST EASY BEACHES

🏖️ Ammes + Avithos

For young children, practical beaches matter more than postcard fame. Ammes is known for shallow, calmer water; Avithos combines sand, relatively gentle entry and nearby food. Both are much easier family choices than building the day around Myrtos.

EASY OUTING

🐢 Argostoli Lagoon

A calm morning or late-afternoon alternative to another beach. The lagoon area offers gentle boating and the possibility of seeing Caretta-caretta turtles, while Argostoli gives adults cafés and an easy promenade.

FAMILY ADVENTURE

🫏 Donkey walk near Sami

A countryside outing with donkeys can be a memorable change of pace. Keep it to a shorter morning experience for the younger children and confirm the operator’s current age, riding and weight rules before booking.

RAIN / HEAT OPTION

🪨 Melissani + Drogarati

The caves add novelty without requiring a full day in direct sun. Pair them with lunch in Sami rather than adding multiple distant stops.

Note: Drogarati involves steps and uneven surfaces. Decide based on the youngest children and anyone with mobility concerns.

SPECIAL DAY

⛵ Family boat day

A private or family-oriented cruise can work particularly well for a multigenerational group because the day comes to you: swimming, scenery, lunch and shade without repeated car loading and unloading.

For this group: prioritize shade, easy boarding, toilet facilities, flotation gear for children and a captain/crew rather than a self-drive boat.

🚫 Myrtos with young children?

Visit the viewpoint for the iconic family photograph, but treat a Myrtos swim as optional. The beach is exposed, pebbly and can have currents. For a relaxed family swim, choose Ammes, Avithos or an organized Lassi beach instead.

☀️ The family-week rhythm

Morning: one outing. Midday: lunch, pool, naps and shade. Late afternoon: second swim or simple activity. Evening: dinner together when it works.

The children do not need an attraction every day. Villa Astoria itself should remain part of the vacation.
