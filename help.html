⚠️ Food allergy card

This quick card is designed to be shown directly to restaurant staff.

Έχω σοβαρή αλλεργία στα φιστίκια (αράπικα φιστίκια). Παρακαλώ μην χρησιμοποιήσετε φιστίκια, φυστικοβούτυρο ή τρόφιμα που έχουν έρθει σε επαφή με φιστίκια.

English: I have a serious peanut allergy. Please do not use peanuts, peanut butter, or food that has come into contact with peanuts.

Before travel: this wording should be reviewed for the guest who will use it and saved as a screenshot/offline card. Restaurant staff should also be told verbally about the allergy.
