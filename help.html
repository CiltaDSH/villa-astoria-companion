🚨 Emergency help🏥 Medical care🏡 Practical help

If there is immediate danger, serious injury, severe breathing difficulty, loss of consciousness or another potentially life-threatening situation, call 112 rather than trying to solve transportation first.

🚨 Emergency

112 is the European emergency number and the simplest first call for urgent emergency assistance in Greece. It is free, operates 24/7, and can be called from a mobile phone even without a SIM card. If your own mobile network has no coverage, another available network may carry the 112 call.

English is available: Greece’s 112 operators answer in Greek, English and French.

📞 Call 112Ambulance / EKAV 166
Useful to tell the operator: what happened, how many people need help, your current location, and a callback number. If you are at Villa Astoria, use the saved precise Villa pin whenever possible; “Ai Helis / Svoronata, Kefalonia” is useful additional context.

166 is the direct EKAV ambulance number. It is included as a secondary reference; when the nature of an emergency or the help required is uncertain, use 112 first.
