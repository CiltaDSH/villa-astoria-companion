Kefalonia is already the gathering. Nobody needs another island in order to have “done enough.”

This page is less a list of places to collect than a way to decide whether leaving Kefalonia makes sense at all. For most shorter Villa Astoria stays, the best answer will be: enjoy the island you came to see.

OFTEN THE BEST CHOICE

🏡 Stay on Kefalonia

For guests with only three or four days at Villa Astoria, an off-island trip usually trades too much time for ports, schedules and transfers. Kefalonia itself has more than enough variety for a short stay: beaches, villages, caves, mountain country, wine, boats and long meals.

A useful test: if leaving Kefalonia means giving up the relaxed day you actually wanted here, stay.

BEST NEIGHBORING-ISLAND FIT

⛴️ Ithaca

If someone staying at Villa Astoria wants one neighboring-island experience, Ithaca is the most natural choice. Its appeal is not simply that it is another island; it offers a quieter, distinct Ionian character close enough to make the idea reasonable.

A day trip can work when ferry timing is favorable. An overnight makes more sense for someone who wants to experience Ithaca itself rather than spend a large part of the day watching the clock.

CHOOSE FOR A SPECIFIC REASON

🚤 Zakynthos

Zakynthos is possible from Kefalonia, but it asks more of the day than Ithaca. It makes sense when a guest has a particular reason to go—not simply because another island can be added.

Think first: port transfers, seasonal ferry timing and the return journey can turn this into a logistics-heavy day. If the attraction is mainly another beautiful beach or boat ride, Kefalonia may provide the easier answer.

A DIFFERENT KIND OF CHOICE

🧳 Continue elsewhere after Villa Astoria

Travel after the gathering is different from taking a day away from it. Guests with additional vacation time may reasonably use Kefalonia as one chapter in a longer trip—another Ionian island, mainland Greece or a city elsewhere in the region.

The key distinction: do not force a major destination into a Villa Astoria day trip when it would be better enjoyed as the next stop on the journey.

That approach preserves the relaxed character of the stay and gives the onward destination enough time to be worthwhile.

🧭 The gathering rule

Short stay: stay on Kefalonia.
Longer stay and genuinely curious about a neighboring island: consider Ithaca.
Specific interest in Zakynthos: go deliberately, after checking the logistics.
Major destination farther away: make it an onward trip rather than a spoke from Villa Astoria.
