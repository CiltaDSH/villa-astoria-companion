LOWEST LOGISTICS

🏡 Stay on Kefalonia

For guests with only three or four days at Villa Astoria, an off-island trip can consume a meaningful share of the stay in ports, schedules and transfers. Kefalonia itself already offers beaches, villages, caves, mountain country, wine, boats and long meals.

Worth asking: does leaving the island add something you genuinely want, or mostly add movement?

BEST NEIGHBORING-ISLAND FIT

⛴️ Ithaca

If someone staying at Villa Astoria wants one neighboring-island experience, Ithaca is the most natural choice. Its appeal is not simply that it is another island; it offers a quieter, distinct Ionian character close enough to make the idea reasonable.

A day trip can work when ferry timing is favorable. An overnight makes more sense for someone who wants to experience Ithaca itself rather than organize the day around the return sailing.

CHOOSE FOR A SPECIFIC REASON

🚤 Zakynthos

Zakynthos is possible from Kefalonia, but it asks more of the day than Ithaca. It makes the most sense when a guest has a particular reason to go rather than simply wanting to add another island.

The tradeoff: port transfers, seasonal ferry timing and the return journey can make this a logistics-heavy outing. If the attraction is mainly another beautiful beach or boat ride, Kefalonia may offer the easier version.

A DIFFERENT KIND OF CHOICE

🧳 Continue elsewhere after Villa Astoria

Travel after the gathering is different from taking a day away from it. Guests with additional vacation time may reasonably use Kefalonia as one chapter in a longer trip. The question is less “what else can I fit in?” than “what would make a genuinely different and worthwhile next chapter?”

Often the cleaner choice: if a destination deserves several days, give it those days after Villa Astoria rather than squeezing it into the gathering.

🌊 Stay in the Ionian world

Ithaca, Lefkada, Corfu or another Ionian stop can suit someone who wants to remain near the water and continue at an island pace. The character changes from island to island, but this is the most natural continuation for a guest who is not ready to leave the Ionian setting behind.

🏛️ See another side of Greece

Athens—or a longer mainland-Greece journey—creates a more pronounced change of scene: history, museums, neighborhoods, archaeological sites and city life rather than another variation on an island stay. Athens is also the principal air hub for many onward journeys from Kefalonia. Those are two different reasons to be there: a connection through Athens does not require adding an Athens stay, while choosing Athens as a destination deserves enough time to enjoy the city on its own terms.

🌍 Make it a larger regional extension

For guests with more time, a city elsewhere in the region can become a distinct second trip rather than an add-on to Kefalonia. Istanbul is one example: culturally and visually different enough to deserve its own several-day stay. Other European or Mediterranean cities may fit just as well depending on a guest’s interests and flight connections.

Think in connections, not just distance: some Ionian destinations may make sense by seasonal ferry; mainland Greece can involve ferry-and-road travel or a flight; farther destinations will usually be simplest by air, often connecting through Athens. Exact routes and schedules should be checked for the traveler’s actual 2027 dates.
Also count the recovery cost: a destination involving several transfers may be entirely worthwhile for a longer stay, but much less appealing for only a night or two. Consider how much of the next chapter you want to spend moving, unpacking and recovering from travel.

A useful rule: choose an onward destination because you want to spend meaningful time there—not because it happens to be geographically nearby.

🧭 A simple way to think about it

Short stay: Kefalonia itself may be the better use of the time.
Longer stay and genuinely curious about a neighboring island: Ithaca is the most natural fit.
Specific interest in Zakynthos: the extra logistics may still be worth it.
Major destination farther away: it will usually work better as an onward trip than as a Villa Astoria excursion.
