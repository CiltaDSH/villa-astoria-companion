🏡ZERO LOGISTICS

Stay at the hub

Pool, book, conversation, lunch, nap, another swim. This is not the fallback plan—it is one of the reasons we chose a villa.

🏖️EASY

I want a beach

Walk to Ai Helis or make the short drive to Avithos. Keep the north-coast icons for a day when the drive itself sounds appealing.

🏙️LOW EFFORT

I want to get out—but not all day

Argostoli, a café, shopping, waterfront stroll or dinner can change the scenery without consuming the day.

⭐SIGNATURE

I want to see “Kefalonia”

Choose one defining combination: Myrtos + Assos or Melissani + Sami. One memorable anchor is enough.

🌲HOT DAY

I want less direct sun

Caves, a shaded lunch, Mount Ainos or simply the villa can be smarter than another exposed midday beach.

🍽️FOOD

I want today to revolve around a meal

Pick the dinner first, then build a modest day around Avithos, a winery, Argostoli or sunset near the water.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦WITH CHILDREN

We need an easy family win

Short beach, turtles, caves, pool time or a crewed boat day. Avoid stacking several attractions into one hot day.

⛵ADVENTURE

I want something special

Boat experience, wine outing, north-island drive—or, if the day truly warrants it, a neighboring island.
