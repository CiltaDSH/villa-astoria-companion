😌 I want a slow Villa Astoria day

Staying at the villa is not the absence of a plan. It can be one of the best ways to enjoy the gathering.

Villa + pool + whatever happens

zero guiltthe hub itself

Coffee, book, pool, conversation, lunch, nap, drink, dinner—or some entirely different sequence. A full day at Villa Astoria needs no justification and no sightseeing attached to it.

A short escape, if it appeals

shortlow effort

Ai Helis, Avithos, Argostoli, a nearby winery or an evening walk can add a little island without claiming the day—and can just as easily be skipped.

Repeating something you loved is a perfectly good use of a day.

If Avithos becomes your beach, go back. If Assos calls for a second visit, return. There is no reason to replace something you genuinely enjoyed with another sight simply because it remains unchecked.
